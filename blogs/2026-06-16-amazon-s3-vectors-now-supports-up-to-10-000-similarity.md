Amazon S3 Vectors increases similarity search limits to 10,000 results, a 100x increase over the previous limit, suiting multi-stage retrieval pipelines that apply reranking, aggregations, or deduplication. Results paginate automatically, with the first 512 KB per query free and data-returned fees applied thereafter.
