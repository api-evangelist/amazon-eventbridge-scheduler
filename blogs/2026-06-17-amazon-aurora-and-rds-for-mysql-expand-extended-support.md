Amazon RDS extends MySQL 5.7 support through June 2029, offering security patches for critical and high CVEs, bug fixes for critical operational issues, and access to AWS Support within standard Aurora and RDS SLAs. No price increase applies, and Blue/Green Deployments simplify upgrades to MySQL 8.0 or 8.4.
