AgentCore introduces optimization capabilities surfacing failure, intent, and trajectory insights across hundreds of sessions to reveal patterns no dashboard or one-at-a-time trace review would catch. Recommendations generate data-grounded fixes, batch evaluation tests changes against defined datasets, and A/B testing validates improvements in production.
