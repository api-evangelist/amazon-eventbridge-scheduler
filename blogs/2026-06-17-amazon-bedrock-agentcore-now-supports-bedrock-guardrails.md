AgentCore now integrates Bedrock Guardrails in policy, evaluating outputs of every authorized agent action and inputs of every call to a gateway target (tools, agents, and models) in real time. Guardrails detect and block prompt injection attacks, harmful content, and sensitive information exposure at the perimeter.
