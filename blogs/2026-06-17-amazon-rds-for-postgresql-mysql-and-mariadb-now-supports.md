Graviton5-based M9g instances launch for RDS PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MariaDB, delivering up to 30% performance improvement and up to 23% price/performance improvement for on-demand pricing. New 24xlarge and 48xlarge sizes offer up to 192 vCPU, 100Gbps networking, and 72Gbps EBS bandwidth, available in US East, US West, and Europe (Frankfurt).
