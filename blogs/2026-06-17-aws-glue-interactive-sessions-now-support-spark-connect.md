AWS Glue Interactive Sessions now support Apache Spark Connect, enabling development from preferred environments including managed notebooks in Amazon SageMaker Unified Studio, or other notebook environments and IDEs. The thin client architecture decouples applications from execution environments for ad hoc exploration and debugging.
