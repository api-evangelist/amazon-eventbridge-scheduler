AWS HealthOmics streams workflow engine logs to CloudWatch in real time, accelerating iterative workflow development and debugging by giving researchers and bioinformaticians immediate access to execution details during a run. Logs include orchestration events, task scheduling, import/export activity, and error traces for Nextflow, WDL, and CWL workflows.
