title: "AWS Outposts racks now support bmn-cx3a instances, the first AMD-based instances with accelerated networking on Outposts"
url: "https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/06/aws-outposts-amd-bmn-cx3a/"
date: "2026-06-17"
feed_url: "https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/recent/feed/"
---
AWS Outposts racks now support bmn-cx3a instances featuring 5th Gen AMD EPYC processors with a maximum frequency of 4.1 GHz and NVIDIA ConnectX-7 (CX7) network interface cards, delivering up to 800 Gbps of bare-metal accelerated network bandwidth. Sizes include bmn-cx3a.metal-32xl and bmn-cx3a.metal-64xl with native L2 multicast and PTP support.
