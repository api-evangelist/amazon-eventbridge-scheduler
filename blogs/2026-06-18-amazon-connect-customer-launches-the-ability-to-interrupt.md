title: "Amazon Connect Customer launches the ability to interrupt an agent with an urgent contact"

date: "2026-06-18"
feed_url: "https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/recent/feed/"
---
Amazon Connect now allows interrupting agents with urgent contacts, overriding their usual routing configuration so agents can receive urgent calls even while on other calls. The feature also enables direct assignment to agents in custom statuses normally unavailable for queued contacts.
