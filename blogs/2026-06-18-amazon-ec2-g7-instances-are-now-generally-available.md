EC2 G7 instances, powered by NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs, deliver up to 4.6x AI inference performance and up to 2.1x graphics performance compared to G6. They support up to 8 GPUs with 32GB memory each, custom Intel Xeon 6 processors, and 700 Gbps EFA networking, available in US East (Ohio) and US West (Oregon).
