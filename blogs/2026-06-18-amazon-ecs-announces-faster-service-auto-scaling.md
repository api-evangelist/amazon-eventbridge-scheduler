ECS service auto scaling now supports high resolution (20-second) metrics, reducing scale-out time from 363 to 86 seconds (76% faster). Target tracking policies for CPU and memory utilization can use 20-second resolution alongside the default 60-second option, working across Fargate, Managed Instances, and EC2 compute options.
