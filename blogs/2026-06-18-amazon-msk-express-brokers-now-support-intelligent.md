Amazon MSK Provisioned clusters with Express brokers now support Intelligent Rebalancing at no additional cost on all existing clusters, performing operations up to 180 times faster compared to Standard brokers. It automatically balances Kafka resources, optimizes capacity utilization, and scales brokers without affecting client availability.
