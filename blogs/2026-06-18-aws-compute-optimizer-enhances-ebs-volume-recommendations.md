AWS Compute Optimizer now analyzes VolumeIOPSExceededCheck and VolumeThroughputExceededCheck metrics for EBS volume rightsizing recommendations, factoring in performance spikes to balance cost with performance for bursty workloads. Available in all AWS regions except GovCloud and China.
