Nested virtualization is expanding to additional Intel instance types and US GovCloud regions, with supported platforms now including C7i, R7i, M7i, C8id, R8id, M8id, C7i-flex, M7i-flex, I7i, C8i-flex, R8i-flex, M8i-flex, and X8i. Users can create nested environments by running KVM or Hyper-V on virtual EC2 instances for emulators, vehicle hardware simulation, and Windows Subsystem for Linux.
